DriverWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy describes how Own Path Digital handles information in the current DriverWise / Delivery Earnings Lab prototype (version 0.04). DriverWise is a delivery-driver earnings and profitability tracking application under development.

Information you enter

The app allows you to enter earnings, tips, mileage readings, fuel, insurance and other costs, and shift details. These details are used to calculate earnings and profitability on your device. In the current prototype, some shift information is saved locally on your device using app preferences; other entered values may not persist after closing the app.

Images and text recognition

If you choose to import an earnings screenshot, the app accesses the image you select using the Android image picker. It uses Google ML Kit text recognition to extract figures for you to review and correct. Text recognition is performed on your device. The current prototype does not implement a feature to upload these screenshots or extracted earnings figures to an Own Path Digital server.

Accounts, advertising and location

The current prototype does not require you to create an account, does not display advertising and does not implement GPS tracking or notification-reading automation. These may be considered for later versions; this policy will be updated before such features are released.

Sharing and third-party services

Own Path Digital does not operate a server that receives the earnings and shift details entered into the current prototype. The app uses Flutter and third-party software libraries, including Google ML Kit, for its functionality. Any data processing by the Android operating system, app distribution service, or third-party libraries is subject to their applicable practices. We will review the final release build and its dependencies before publishing updated disclosures.

Retention and deletion

Some shift details are stored locally on your device. You can remove locally stored app data using Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to any device backup or operating-system retention behavior. The current prototype does not offer a cloud account or server-side data deletion process.

Security

The current prototype is designed to process earnings information locally. No method of storage is completely risk-free. Protect your device using its available security features.

Children

DriverWise is intended for adult delivery drivers and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes, particularly if optional location tracking, notification access, subscriptions or other features are introduced. The updated policy will be posted at this address with a revised date.
